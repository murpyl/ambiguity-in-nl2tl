## Exclusions and limitations

- The supplied or_scope seed `(G(a -> F b)) | (G(a -> F c))` versus `G(a -> F(b | c))` was tested with Spot and found equivalent on infinite traces. It was excluded. The family remains a candidate mechanism, using contexts such as immediate or next-step responses and response windows that yield inequivalent readings.
- trigger_scope initially generated 12 items, then excluded 005 and 006: their formulas differed only in trigger existence. Final source contains 10 items, with intentional ID gaps. See trigger_scope_final/generation_notes.md for formulas and reasoning.
- trigger_scope and order_scope overlap in existential versus universal trigger scope. The already-used immediate-response contrast was omitted from order_scope, but the mechanisms are not claimed to be independent.
- Each family stopped below 15 rather than padding weak structures. No mechanism was replaced with a new one; human review has not established which mechanisms should be retained.
- Solver eligibility establishes inequivalent formulas within each record. The commands do not establish linguistic ambiguity, formula-to-English fidelity, clear-control quality, or full cross-record semantic diversity under renaming.
- No human acceptance, control selection, final dataset export, or 200-row quota fulfillment is claimed. The target remains 100 accepted contrast records plus one selected clear control per record.
- The existing dataset_checks.py documentation describes a different final-row accounting (one control per endorsed reading). Only its candidates stage was run; its final-stage policy was not changed or relied upon.

No scripts, dependencies, configuration, or database state were changed.
