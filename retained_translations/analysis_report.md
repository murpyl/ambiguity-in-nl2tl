# Retained translation correlation results

Analysis completed on November 7, 2026. All five models have 183 complete statements: 59 ambiguous inputs and 124 clear semantic-group representatives, with 10 samples per statement. All 9,150 samples were included. Each model has 59 complete paired comparisons across eight families.

## Semantic diversity and accuracy

Class counts group translations by exact Spot semantic equivalence. Accuracy below is per sampled translation, not majority-vote accuracy. Paired differences compare each ambiguous input against the mean of its own clear representatives. The clear mean in the table instead averages all 124 clear statements, so its difference from the ambiguous mean need not equal the paired difference.

| Model | Classes: clear | Classes: ambiguous | Paired class difference | Paired p | Accuracy: clear | Accuracy: ambiguous |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Claude Haiku | 1.185 | 1.542 | +0.347 | 0.00484 | 88.4% | 85.9% |
| DeepSeek | 1.218 | 1.525 | +0.299 | 0.00243 | 91.2% | 89.2% |
| Gemini | 1.081 | 1.288 | +0.203 | 0.00472 | 94.7% | 92.5% |
| GPT Luna | 1.210 | 1.390 | +0.172 | 0.045 | 87.0% | 89.0% |
| Qwen | 1.250 | 1.763 | +0.503 | 2.61e-06 | 87.7% | 88.8% |

All five models produce more semantic classes on ambiguous inputs. Qwen has the largest observed gap. The class-count tests are nominally significant for all five models; applying a Bonferroni correction across these five tests retains four results, with GPT Luna no longer significant. This does not correct for every metric explored, or for dependence within families.

Entropy is higher and majority share is lower on ambiguous inputs for every model. GPT Luna has weaker paired evidence: entropy p=0.054 and majority-share p=0.075. Gemini has the highest observed sample-level accuracy in both input categories; this is a descriptive comparison, not a tested model ranking.

## Does diversity reflect multiple retained readings?

Among the 59 ambiguous inputs, the numbers yielding at least two distinct retained interpretations within ten samples are: Claude Haiku 5, DeepSeek 14, Gemini 12, GPT Luna 8, and Qwen 22. The numbers yielding multiple semantic classes of any kind are respectively 16, 24, 15, 18, and 32. Thus diversity often includes formulas outside the retained gold set rather than exploration of multiple accepted readings. Outside-set formulas are not necessarily linguistically indefensible: the retained set may be incomplete.

Gemini has the cleanest observed correspondence: 12 of its 15 diverse ambiguous inputs include multiple retained readings. Qwen covers multiple retained readings for the most inputs, but also produces out-of-set formulas on 16 ambiguous inputs.

## Interpretation and limits

Many ambiguous inputs still yield a single semantic class: 27–44 out of 59, depending on model. Semantic consistency therefore cannot establish that an input is unambiguous. Conversely, clear inputs sometimes yield multiple classes, so diversity alone cannot establish ambiguity.

Semantic-class count correlates negatively with translation accuracy (Spearman rho approximately -0.49 to -0.85 across models). This is an association: diversity can reflect translation difficulty as well as ambiguity. There are also consistently wrong clear inputs for every model, so agreement does not establish correctness.

Paired accuracy differences are not statistically significant for any model. Clear and ambiguous accuracy are not equally demanding tasks: ambiguous outputs can match any retained interpretation, while clear outputs have one target. The same models participated in constructing the verified interpretation set, so these scores are not independent human-ground-truth evaluations.

Only 10 samples are available per input, limiting discovery of rare interpretations. Pair-level p-values are exploratory because pairs share eight ambiguity families. There were 10 unparseable outputs: eight DeepSeek and two GPT Luna. Constant-input warnings arise because parse-failure counts are zero for the other three models; their parse-failure correlations are undefined, not failed analysis runs.

## Reproduction

```sh
/opt/miniconda3/envs/ltl/bin/python analyze_correlation_retained.py
```

Per-model metrics and paired CSVs are generated by the script. `analysis_summary.csv` and this report summarize those outputs plus an additional Spot-equivalence check of retained-reading coverage. `correlation_analysis.log` preserves the full console output. No translation API calls were made and no source files were changed.
